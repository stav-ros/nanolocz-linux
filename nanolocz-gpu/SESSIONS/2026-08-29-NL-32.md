## Specification

Created `SPEC/NL-32-particle-substacks.md` with full acceptance criteria:

1. **Substack extraction function** — Extract fixed-size patches centered on detected particles
2. **Drift-aware extraction** — Apply inverse drift during extraction without full-movie resampling
3. **Mask-based extraction** — Support binary and soft masks for particle region isolation
4. **Batch processing** — Efficient multi-particle extraction with progress callbacks
5. **GPU acceleration** — CuPy-accelerated parallel extraction with CPU fallback
6. **Integration** — Export from `nanolocz.core.substacks`, compatible with Zarr storage
